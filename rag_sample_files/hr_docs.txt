ZORVEX TECHNOLOGIES — EMPLOYEE DOCUMENTATION AND HR RECORDS POLICY
Policy ID HR-OPS-004 | Version 2.0 | Effective 1 November 2026
Owner: HR Operations

1. PURPOSE
This guide covers documents collected during recruitment, onboarding, employment, payroll, benefits, and separation. It is fictional training content; actual requirements depend on law, role, and employing entity.

2. PRE-JOINING DOCUMENTS
Depending on the role and legal requirements, HR may request identity/address proof, photograph, education certificates, professional licences, previous employment/relieving letters, signed offer and employment agreement, tax declarations, bank details, emergency contact, and benefit nomination forms. Collect only information needed for a defined purpose.

3. IDENTITY AND ADDRESS VERIFICATION
Submit accepted documents through the official secure HR portal. HR checks name and relevant details and asks the employee to resolve discrepancies. Do not retain original identity documents unless legally required. Never post identity numbers in public channels.

4. EDUCATION AND EMPLOYMENT VERIFICATION
Where relevant, an approved provider may verify degrees, certificates, employment dates, titles, and credentials. Inform the employee and provide a fair opportunity to explain material discrepancies. Reports are confidential and access-controlled.

5. PAYROLL AND TAX RECORDS
Employees provide bank details, tax declarations, and legally required payroll information through the approved payroll system. Update changes promptly. HR/Payroll must restrict access to salary, tax, and bank records. HR will never ask for bank PINs, passwords, or OTPs.

6. STATUTORY AND BENEFIT ENROLLMENT
HR may collect information required for provident fund, employee state insurance, gratuity, professional tax, insurance, nominee, and other statutory/benefit administration. Applicability depends on establishment coverage, wage thresholds, employee category, and current law. Do not assume every scheme applies to every employee.

7. EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT AND POLICY ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS
The agreement may specify role, compensation, location, working hours, probation, notice period, confidentiality, intellectual property, and employment conditions. Employees may acknowledge Code of Conduct, information security, acceptable use, privacy, anti-harassment, leave, and expense policies.

8. EMERGENCY CONTACTS AND NOMINEES
Provide an emergency contact and update it when needed. Complete statutory or benefit nominations where required. Use this information only for legitimate emergency or administrative purposes.

9. PERSONAL INFORMATION CHANGES
Notify HR of legal name, address, phone, bank account, tax status, dependant, nominee, or emergency-contact changes. Submit supporting evidence securely. HR verifies and confirms the update.

10. DOCUMENT SECURITY
Use only the HR portal or a verified HR representative. Do not send sensitive documents through public chat, personal email, or unverified links. HR applies role-based access, encryption where available, audit logs, and secure deletion.

11. CORRECTIONS AND EMPLOYEE ACCESS
Raise a ticket identifying the incorrect field and supporting evidence. HR should acknowledge within 3 working days and provide an expected resolution date. Employees must not alter official records or forge, backdate, or falsify documents. Access requests are handled subject to law and security checks.

12. CONFIDENTIALITY AND RETENTION
Employment, identity, payroll, medical, disciplinary, and background-check records have restricted access. Retain records only for legitimate employment, statutory, audit, and dispute-resolution periods under the records schedule.

13. EMPLOYEE SELF-SERVICE
The HR portal may provide payslips, tax statements, leave balances, holiday calendars, policy acknowledgements, personal details, and benefit enrollment. Report missing or incorrect data to HR Operations.

14. SEPARATION DOCUMENTS
At exit, HR coordinates resignation acceptance, notice records, asset return, access removal, final settlement, tax documents, experience/relieving letter where applicable, and benefit closure. Timing and deductions follow applicable law and contract.

15. ESCALATION
For portal access, corrections, payroll records, or verification questions, raise an HR Operations ticket. Report suspected identity theft or unauthorized disclosure immediately through the security incident channel.
