COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE: HR DOCUMENTS
Last updated: 15 October 2026
Owner: Human Resources

1. Purpose
This fictional guide lists common documents requested during employee onboarding.

2. Documents commonly required
- Government-issued identity and address proof, where legally permitted and required.
- Tax and payroll forms applicable to the employee's location.
- Bank account details for salary payment, submitted through the approved secure HR channel.
- Education or professional certificates when relevant to the role.
- Signed offer letter, employment agreement, and required policy acknowledgements.
- Emergency contact details.

3. Submission rules
Employees should upload documents to the official HR portal or submit them directly to an authorized HR representative. Do not send sensitive documents to unknown email addresses or public chat channels.

4. Corrections
If a document contains an error, contact HR and request a correction. Do not alter official documents yourself.

5. Privacy
HR documents contain personal information. Access should be limited to authorized personnel with a legitimate business need. This sample does not represent legal advice or a real company's policy.
