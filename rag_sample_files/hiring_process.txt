COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE: HIRING PROCESS
Last updated: 15 October 2026
Owner: Talent Acquisition

1. Purpose
This document explains the standard hiring process for full-time employees at ExampleTech. It is a fictional sample policy created for RAG learning.

2. Hiring stages
- Role approval: The hiring manager submits a role request with the job description, level, budget, and business justification.
- Recruiter screening: Talent Acquisition reviews applications and conducts an initial discussion about experience, availability, and compensation expectations.
- Technical or role assessment: Candidates complete a role-relevant interview, exercise, or assessment.
- Interview panel: The hiring manager and panel assess skills using the same role criteria.
- Reference and background checks: These checks are conducted only with the candidate's consent and according to applicable law.
- Offer approval: Compensation and role details must be approved before an offer is sent.
- Onboarding: After acceptance, HR coordinates documentation, equipment, and the joining schedule.

3. Typical timeline
A standard hiring cycle may take 2–4 weeks, but timelines vary by role, interview availability, and background checks. This is an estimate, not a guarantee.

4. Candidate communication
Recruiters should share the next step and an estimated response date after each interview. Candidates may contact their recruiter for a status update.

5. Exceptions
Urgent hiring or senior roles may use a modified process if Talent Acquisition and the hiring manager approve it.
