COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE: TECHNICAL SUPPORT AND ACCESS
Last updated: 15 October 2026
Owner: IT Operations

1. Account access
Use single sign-on (SSO) for supported company applications. Enable multi-factor authentication (MFA) wherever required. Never share passwords, recovery codes, or authentication tokens.

2. Access requests
Submit access requests through the IT service desk. Include the application name, business justification, required role, manager approval when applicable, and the duration for temporary access.

3. Troubleshooting
- Check the service status page for a known outage.
- Confirm network connectivity and whether the issue affects one application or several.
- Capture the exact error message and timestamp.
- Avoid posting secrets, customer data, or access tokens in tickets.
- If the issue persists, open a service desk ticket with safe diagnostic details.

4. Incident severity
P1: Critical service unavailable for many users or major business operations.
P2: Major degradation with a significant workaround or limited scope.
P3: Individual or limited-impact issue with a reasonable workaround.
P4: General question or low-priority request.

5. Security incidents
Report suspected phishing, lost devices, exposed credentials, or unauthorized access to the security team immediately using the official incident reporting channel.

6. Change management
Production changes require an approved change record, a rollback plan, and appropriate testing unless an authorized emergency process applies.
