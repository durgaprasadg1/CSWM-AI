ZORVEX TECHNOLOGIES — IT OPERATIONS AND INFORMATION SECURITY POLICY
Policy ID IT-SEC-006 | Version 2.0 | Effective 1 November 2026
Owner: IT and Information Security

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy covers accounts, access, devices, data handling, email, remote work, software development, incidents, backups, vendors, and production changes. Fictional RAG training content.

2. IDENTITY AND ACCESS
Use assigned corporate identity and SSO. MFA is mandatory on supported systems. Shared accounts are prohibited unless formally approved and controlled. Never share passwords, recovery codes, API keys, session cookies, or OTPs.

3. ACCESS REQUESTS
Submit application, business justification, requested role, duration, data classification, and manager approval through the service desk. System owners approve least-privilege access. Privileged access needs additional approval and stronger authentication. Temporary access must expire automatically.

4. JOINER, MOVER, LEAVER
HR triggers onboarding and separation workflows. IT provisions approved access, adjusts permissions on role changes, and promptly disables accounts at separation or authorized instruction. Managers must report transfers and departures. Rotate exposed secrets immediately.

5. PASSWORDS AND MFA
Use unique passwords and an approved password manager. Do not reuse corporate passwords on personal sites. Reject unexpected MFA prompts. Report repeated prompts, phishing, or account lockouts. IT will never ask for passwords or OTPs in chat.

6. DEVICE SECURITY
Company devices must use supported operating systems, screen locks, disk encryption, endpoint protection, and security updates. Do not disable controls or install unapproved software. Report lost/stolen devices immediately for session revocation or authorized remote wipe.

7. DATA CLASSIFICATION
Public: approved for public release.
Internal: routine information for employees and approved partners.
Confidential: business plans, non-public financial data, customer contracts, operational information.
Restricted: credentials, personal data, payment data, security secrets, and regulated information.
Apply suitable access, encryption, sharing, retention, and deletion controls.

8. STORAGE AND SHARING
Use approved Company storage. Share only with intended recipients and minimum permissions. Public links for Confidential/Restricted data are prohibited unless a documented exception is approved. Verify recipients. Do not upload Company data to personal cloud or unapproved AI services.

9. EMAIL AND PHISHING
Be cautious with unexpected attachments, login links, payment instructions, and credential requests. Verify unusual requests using a separate trusted channel. Report suspicious messages using the mail client or Security service desk. Do not forward malicious files to colleagues.

10. NETWORK AND REMOTE WORK
Use approved VPN or zero-trust access where required. Avoid untrusted public Wi-Fi for sensitive work unless approved protection is active. Secure devices physically and do not let others use corporate devices. Report suspected compromise.

11. SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT AND SECRETS
Use approved repositories. Never commit secrets, keys, tokens, or production data. Use a secrets manager and scoped service identities. Review dependencies, scan code, and restrict production access. Security testing must stay within authorized scope.

12. CHANGE AND RELEASE MANAGEMENT
Production changes require an approved change record, risk assessment, testing evidence, deployment/monitoring plan, and rollback procedure. Emergency changes may use expedited approval but must be documented and reviewed afterward. Unapproved direct production changes are prohibited.

13. INCIDENT REPORTING
Immediately report phishing, malware, lost devices, exposed credentials, unauthorized access, data leakage, or unusual activity through the official Security channel. Include time, system, symptoms, and safe evidence. Do not destroy logs or investigate beyond authority.

14. SERVICE DESK PRIORITY
P1: critical outage or active broad-impact security incident.
P2: major degradation or important function unavailable.
P3: limited impact with workaround.
P4: general question or low-priority request.
Security incidents may be escalated independently of normal priority.

15. BACKUP AND RECOVERY
System owners define backup frequency, retention, encryption, RPO, and RTO. Restrict backup access and test restoration periodically. A successful backup job alone does not prove recoverability.

16. THIRD-PARTY ACCESS
Vendor access needs a business owner, contract/approval, least privilege, expiry, and monitoring. Disable access when work ends or the agreement expires.

17. ACCEPTABLE USE AND EXCEPTIONS
Limited personal use may be allowed if lawful, secure, and non-disruptive. Harassment, unlawful content, piracy, bypassing controls, fraud, and unauthorized monitoring are prohibited. Exceptions require documented justification, risk acceptance, compensating controls, and expiry date. Violations may result in access suspension or disciplinary review.
