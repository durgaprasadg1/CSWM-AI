COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE: LEAVE POLICY
Last updated: 15 October 2026
Owner: Human Resources

1. Scope
This is a fictional example leave policy for demonstrating retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).

2. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 18 days of annual leave per calendar year, prorated for eligible employees who join during the year. Local law or an employment agreement may provide different entitlements.

3. Sick leave
Employees may use up to 8 days of sick leave per calendar year under this sample policy. A medical certificate may be requested for absences longer than 3 consecutive working days, subject to applicable law.

4. Public holidays
The company publishes a location-specific holiday calendar each year. Employees should consult the HR portal for the calendar applicable to their work location.

5. Request and approval
- Submit planned leave through the HR portal.
- For planned leave of 3 or more consecutive working days, provide at least 5 working days' notice when practical.
- The manager reviews team coverage and approves or declines the request.
- For unexpected illness, notify the manager as soon as reasonably possible and record the leave when able.

6. Carryover
Unused annual leave may carry over up to 5 days into the next calendar year in this sample policy. Carried-over leave expires on 31 March unless local requirements say otherwise.

7. Important note
These numbers are invented for learning. They must not be treated as actual employment terms.
