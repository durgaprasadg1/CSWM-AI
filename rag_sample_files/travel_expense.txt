COMPANY KNOWLEDGE BASE: TRAVEL AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Last updated: 15 October 2026
Owner: Finance Operations

1. Scope
This fictional policy is intended for testing RAG retrieval and question answering.

2. Before booking
Obtain manager approval before making business travel commitments. Prefer approved booking channels and reasonable, cost-effective options that meet business needs.

3. Example spending limits
- Hotel: up to INR 6,000 per night before taxes, unless an exception is approved.
- Meals during approved business travel: up to INR 1,200 per day.
- Local ground transport: reasonable actual costs with receipts where available.
These are invented sample limits, not real company rules.

4. Expense submission
Submit expenses in the expense portal within 15 calendar days after the trip ends. Include the business purpose, date, category, amount, currency, and itemized receipt when required.

5. Reimbursement
Finance reviews submissions for policy compliance and complete documentation. Incomplete claims may be returned for correction. Approved reimbursements are normally processed in the next scheduled payment cycle.

6. Exceptions
Obtain written approval for expenses above the sample limits before spending when practical. Explain unavoidable exceptions in the claim.

7. Non-reimbursable items
Personal entertainment, personal shopping, traffic fines, and expenses unrelated to business are not reimbursable under this sample policy.
