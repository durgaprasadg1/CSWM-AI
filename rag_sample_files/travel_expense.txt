ZORVEX TECHNOLOGIES — BUSINESS TRAVEL AND EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Policy ID FIN-TRV-007 | Version 2.0 | Effective 1 November 2026
Owner: Finance Operations

1. PURPOSE
Covers travel approval, booking, accommodation, meals, transport, claims, reimbursement, advances, exceptions, and audit. Fictional RAG training content; all sample limits must be replaced by actual approved limits.

2. PRE-TRAVEL APPROVAL
Obtain written manager approval before booking. Request must state business purpose, destination, dates, attendees, estimated cost, project/cost centre, and expected outcome. International travel may require business-head, Finance, Legal, or security approval. Personal extensions must be separated from Company costs.

3. BOOKING PRINCIPLES
Use approved portal/agency where available. Choose safe, reasonable, cost-effective options. Compare total cost, cancellation terms, travel time, and baggage. Do not split bookings to avoid approval thresholds.

4. AIR AND RAIL
Sample default is economy class for domestic and international flights. Premium economy/business class requires documented exception approval for journey duration, medical accommodation, or business necessity. Rail class must be appropriate to duration and safety. Personal upgrades are not reimbursed unless approved.

5. HOTEL
Illustrative domestic hotel ceiling: INR 6,000/night before taxes. Higher cost requires prior written approval. Choose safe accommodation near work location. Minibar, entertainment, spa, guest charges, and personal upgrades are excluded unless approved.

6. MEALS AND INCIDENTALS
Illustrative meal ceiling: INR 1,200 per travel day. Alcohol, personal shopping, tobacco, and non-business guest meals are excluded. Client meals require attendee names, business purpose, itemized receipt, and manager approval. Tips must be reasonable.

7. LOCAL TRANSPORT
Reasonable taxi, rideshare, public transit, airport transfer, and parking may be reimbursed for approved business travel. Personal commuting, traffic fines, and avoidable premium transport are excluded. Obtain receipts where available.

8. PERSONAL VEHICLE
Prior manager approval is required. Employee must hold valid licence and insurance. Illustrative mileage rate: INR 10/km, subject to Finance confirmation. Record date, route, purpose, and distance. Tolls and parking may be claimed separately with receipts.

9. CLAIM SUBMISSION
Submit through expense portal within 15 calendar days after trip end. Each line needs date, merchant, category, amount, currency, business purpose, cost centre, and required receipt. Use approved exchange-rate method. Duplicate claims are prohibited.

10. SUPPORTING DOCUMENTS
Attach itemized invoices, payment proof, booking confirmation, boarding pass where applicable, approval record, and attendee details for business meals. Explain missing receipts and provide alternative evidence. Finance may reject unsupported or illegible claims.

11. REVIEW AND REIMBURSEMENT
Finance checks approvals, policy compliance, arithmetic, receipt quality, duplicates, tax treatment, and cost centre. Incomplete claims may be returned. Approved claims are normally paid in the next scheduled cycle, subject to payroll cut-off and bank processing.

12. ADVANCES AND COMPANY CARDS
Advances require approval and reconciliation within 15 calendar days after return. Company cards are for approved business costs only. Upload receipts and explain unusual transactions. Report lost cards immediately to issuer and Finance.

13. CANCELLATIONS AND CHANGES
Cancel unused bookings promptly. Company-funded refunds and credits belong to the Company. Do not retain travel credits for personal use. Business-related change fees may be reimbursed with approval.

14. NON-REIMBURSABLE COSTS
Personal entertainment/shopping, traffic fines, penalties, tobacco, alcohol unless specifically approved for a permitted business event, companion travel, avoidable no-show fees, and non-business costs are excluded.

15. EXCEPTIONS AND EMERGENCIES
Get written approval before exceeding limits where practical. If emergency prevents prior approval, notify manager and Finance promptly and submit explanation/evidence. Finance records exception decision.

16. FRAUD AND AUDIT
Falsified receipts, duplicate claims, personal expenses presented as business, or misuse of advances may trigger investigation and disciplinary action. Finance may audit claims and request original evidence.

17. TAX AND COMPLIANCE
Provide accurate information for tax/statutory reporting. Finance determines tax treatment under applicable law. Reimbursement is not automatically tax-free.

18. QUICK REFERENCE — FICTIONAL SAMPLE LIMITS
Hotel: INR 6,000/night | Meals: INR 1,200/day | Personal vehicle: INR 10/km
Claim deadline: 15 calendar days after trip completion.
